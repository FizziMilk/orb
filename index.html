Face column

For each turn of the strip, the LED that faces the room. Halves are fine when the front sits between two LEDs. Tick the turn where the eye rests.

  1. Find the LED that faces the room with the finder above.
  2. Go one turn up and down from it and find the LED straight above or below. Enter each one here, bottom turn first.
  3. Check left and right: if the finder's red LED isn't on the left, flip the direction.
