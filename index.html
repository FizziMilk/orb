Quiet and singing parts

Demo song

The orb plays a made-up track into its own detection instead of the mic: calm, build-up, drop, groove, then a short silence, on a loop (about 2.5 minutes). For showing it off quietly or testing effects.

Front line

Where the eye lives: one LED per turn of the strip, facing the room. The orb shows it live while you adjust.

  1. Turn the orb so its front (the IR sensor's side) faces you and press Show.
  2. Move the whole white line until it runs straight up the middle facing you.
  3. Tap a turn to light it blue; nudge any turn that's off the line.
  4. Pick the turn the eye rests on (green), then Save.
